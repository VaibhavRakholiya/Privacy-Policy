Origami Weapons Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application Origami Weapons (package name: com.easy.origami.weapons.craft.guide), published by Epic Lotus Games (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"), as a free service. The application is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects certain information automatically when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • App screens and features you use, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent in the Application
  • The operating system and general device information on your mobile device
  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), used only if and when in-app advertising is enabled
  • App usage and analytics events (for example, which weapon and fold step you view, and app performance events)

Weapon Catalog and Fold Instructions

All weapon folding instructions, step images, and diagrams are bundled inside the Application itself. The Application does not download this content from a server, and you do not upload any content to us through the Application.

Local Preferences and Progress

The Application stores small preferences locally on your device — for example, your light/dark theme choice and whether you have dismissed the one-time zoom hint bubble in the fold player — to improve your experience. This information is not transmitted to our servers.

Device Permissions

The Application may request the following permissions:

  • Internet — to display advertisements (when enabled) and send analytics events
  • Vibrate — for optional haptic feedback during interactions

You can deny permissions in your device settings. Some features may not work without the relevant permission.

The Application does not require you to create an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personally identifiable information to use the core features of the Application.
