Font Downloader Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application Font Downloader (package name: com.font.downloader.picker), published by Epic Lotus Games (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"), as a free service. The application is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects certain information automatically when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • App screens and features you use, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent in the Application
  • The operating system and general device information on your mobile device
  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) used to deliver and measure ads
  • App usage and analytics events (for example, ad load, impression, and performance events)

Font Catalog

The Application downloads a catalog of Google Fonts families from the Google Fonts Web API over the Internet. This content includes font family names, categories, language subsets, and download URLs for font files. The Application reads this catalog only; you do not upload fonts or font metadata to our servers through the Application.

Font Downloads

When you download a font, the Application fetches the font file (for example, a TTF file) from Google's font servers and saves it to your device's Downloads folder. Downloaded files remain on your device unless you delete them. The Service Provider does not receive or store copies of your downloaded font files.

Liked Fonts and Preferences

When you like a font family, the Application stores that preference locally on your device. The Application also stores small preferences locally (for example, liked fonts, custom preview text, theme mode, and in-app review prompt state) to improve your experience. This information is not transmitted to our servers.

Device Permissions

The Application may request the following permissions:

  • Internet — to fetch the font catalog, download font files, display advertisements, and send analytics events
  • Storage — on older Android versions, to save downloaded font files to your Downloads folder

You can deny permissions in your device settings. Some features may not work without the relevant permission.

Accounts and Personal Information

The Application does not require you to create an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personally identifiable information to use the core features of the Application.

Location

The Application does not collect, store, or use your device's location.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes available on your mobile device or via the Google Play Store.

To limit personalized advertising on Android, open Settings > Google > Ads and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (wording may vary by device).

Data Retention Policy

Liked fonts, preview text, and local preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the Application, uninstall the Application, or clear its app data. The Service Provider does not retain copies of your liked fonts or preferences on its servers.

Downloaded font files remain in your device's Downloads folder until you delete them. Analytics and advertising data is retained according to the policies of Google, Firebase, and Yandex.
